PK Battle - Support

PK Battle is a quick turn-based pixel battle game for Apple Watch. It runs fully offline and needs no account.

How to play

  • Choose NEW BATTLE to get a random matchup and pick a move each turn until one side faints.
  • Turn the Digital Crown to move through choices, or use Double Tap where your watch supports it.
  • DEX shows the fighters you have used and beaten.
  • HISTORY lists your past battles and your win/loss record.
  • OPTIONS holds Random Moves, Vibration, Difficulty, Boost Weak and RESET HISTORY.
