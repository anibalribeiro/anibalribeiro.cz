PK Battle - Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Summary

PK Battle for Apple Watch does not collect any personal data. It plays fully offline, has no account or sign-in, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Who is responsible

Aníbal Ribeiro, Prague, Czech Republic. Contact: email@anibalribeiro.cz.

Data on your watch

Battle history, win/loss records, Dex progress and options are saved only on your Apple Watch. The app makes no network requests and never sends gameplay, health or location data to me or anyone else. Use OPTIONS > RESET HISTORY to clear your history, or delete the app to remove all of its data. Backups and purchases are handled by Apple under its own policies.

Website and support

This page sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs, including IP address, requested page, browser information and time, for security and operation. If you email me, I process your email address, your message and any information you choose to include, only to answer your request, and keep the correspondence while it is needed to resolve it.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction or deletion of personal data I hold about you, such as support emails, by writing to email@anibalribeiro.cz. You may also complain to your local data protection authority or the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
