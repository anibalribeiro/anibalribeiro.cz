PK Battle - Support

PK Battle is a quick turn-based pixel battle game for Wear OS. It runs fully offline and needs no account.

How to play

  • Tap NEW BATTLE to get a random matchup, then pick a move each turn until one side faints.
  • Pick a move by tapping it, or by turning the crown or bezel and pressing.
  • RUN opens RUN AWAY? so a stray tap never ends a battle.
  • DEX shows the fighters you have used and beaten.
  • HISTORY lists your past battles and your win/loss record.
  • OPTIONS holds Random Moves, Vibration, Difficulty, Boost Weak and RESET HISTORY.

Free and unlocked

  • 3 New Battles a day are free. The 4th shows DAILY LIMIT with BUY and MENU.
  • The one-time unlock removes the daily limit. Retry, History, Dex and Options are never limited.
  • RESTORE in Options brings the unlock back on the same Google account, for example after a reset or on a new watch.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play, not by me.
