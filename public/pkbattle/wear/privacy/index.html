PK Battle - Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Summary

PK Battle for Wear OS does not collect any personal data. It plays fully offline, has no account or sign-in, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Who is responsible

Aníbal Ribeiro, Prague, Czech Republic. Contact details are on the support page.

Data on your watch

Battle history, the win/loss/draw record, Dex progress, options, the battle in progress, the daily counter for free New Battles and the cached unlock status are saved in one local file on your watch. The game makes no network requests of its own and never sends gameplay, health or location data to me or anyone else.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and I never see your payment details. The app stores only whether the unlock is owned, and re-checks it with Google Play when it starts and when it resumes.

Network and permissions

The app declares the Internet permission because Google Play Billing may exchange diagnostic traffic with Google. The developer receives none of that traffic.

Backup

Android backup or device transfer may copy your history, record and options to your own Google account backup, under Google's terms. It covers history, record and options only, not the battle in progress, the daily counter or the unlock cache.

Deleting your data

Use OPTIONS > RESET HISTORY to clear your history, or clear the app's storage in your watch settings, or uninstall the app, to remove all of its data from the watch. A backup copy in your Google account is managed in your Google account settings.

Website and support

This page sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs, including IP address, requested page, browser information and time, for security and operation. If you email me, I process your email address, your message and any information you choose to include, only to answer your request, and keep the correspondence while it is needed to resolve it.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction or deletion of personal data I hold about you, such as support emails, by emailing me at the address on the support page. You may also complain to your local data protection authority or the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
