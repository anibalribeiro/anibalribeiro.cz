Random PokeBattle - Support

How to play

  • Choose NEW BATTLE to get a random matchup and pick a move each turn until one side faints.
  • Turn the Digital Crown to move through choices, or use Double Tap where your watch supports it.
  • DEX shows the creatures you have used and beaten.
  • HISTORY lists your past battles and your win/loss record.
  • OPTIONS holds settings and RESET HISTORY.
